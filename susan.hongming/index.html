Quan sat 01

Carousel moi la format thuc su cua chi - nhung chi dang dau tu chieu nguoc lai.

Data khong noi doi: carousel trung binh 13,3 likes va 0,79 comments moi bai. Reels chi dat 6,1 likes va 0,25 comments. Nhung trong 100 bai em phan tich, chi dang dang Reels nhieu gap doi carousel (57 vs 29 bai). Chi dang bo nhieu cong suc nhat vao format kem hieu qua nhat voi account cua minh.

Neu chuyen sang carousel-first, engagement se tang gap doi ma khong can them mot follower nao.

Quan sat 02

Nhung Reels hay nhat cua chi dang bi lang phi vi khong co caption.

5 bai duoi cung trong 100 bai: tat ca la Reels, tat ca chi co hashtag, khong co caption. Trong khi bai Reels co nhieu comments nhat (19 likes, 5 comments — DVgRExBAcdT) thi nguoi xem co ly do de o lai vi no co noi dung that su. Reels khong caption = Instagram hieu la chi khong muon truyen tai gi ca.

Them 2-3 cau hook + gia tri + CTA vao moi Reels co the tang views len 50-80 thay vi 11-30.

Quan sat 03

Chi dang ban 3 thu cung luc - va audience khong biet chi la ai.

Shuyi / Life Decoding, Herbalife, Flex45 - ba offer hoan toan khac nhau xuat hien xen ke nhau. Trong khi do, offer doc dao nhat - mat ma ngay sinh / Kinh Dich - lai la offer chi noi it nhat theo format co chieu sau.

Carousel script — 6 slides — song ngu Viet / Anh

Slide 01 — Hook

Ban co bao gio de y rang... Co nhung nguoi sinh ra da gioi quan ly tien bac. Co nguoi lai rat kho giu tien du kiem duoc nhieu. Khong phai do may man. Khong phai do nguoi nay hon nguoi kia. Do con so bi thieu trong ngay sinh cua ho.

Slide 02 — Context

Trong Kinh Dich, moi nguoi sinh ra deu mang theo mot bo so tu ngay thang nam sinh. Nhung khong phai so nao cung co mat. Con so bi thieu - theo Kinh Dich - la nang luong ban chua du manh trong cuoc doi nay. Khong phai diem yeu. La hanh trinh ban duoc sinh ra de hoc. Nothing is random.

Slide 03 — Example: So 1

Thieu so 1 Nang luong can dam & tu chu. Ban co the rat gioi ho tro nguoi khac, nhung thuong kho dua ra quyet dinh cho ban than. Hay de nguoi khac dan dau roi theo sau. Hanh trinh cua ban: hoc tin vao truc giac cua chinh minh.

Slide 04 — Example: So 4

Thieu so 4 Nang luong thuc te & xay dung. Ban co nhieu y tuong, nhung hay quen chuyen thanh hanh dong cu the. Hay thay doi chiec nha trong au tuong nhung chua biet bat dau tu dau. Hanh trinh cua ban: hoc xay tung buoc nho - kien nhan khong co nghia la thu dong.

Slide 05 — CTA

Ban muon biet con so nao bi thieu trong ngay sinh cua minh? De lai ngay / thang sinh o phan binh luan. Minh se gui cho ban mot goi y nho de bat dau giai ma. Because your birthday is not random.
